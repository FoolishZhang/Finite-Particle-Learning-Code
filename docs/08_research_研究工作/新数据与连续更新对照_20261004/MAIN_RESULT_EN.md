# A genuine risk improvement can still receive a worse particle score

We generated 16 new iid datasets of size 4096 from the same prespecified Gaussian–logistic law, with two independent training-prior quartets per dataset. IN4 averages four K100 gradients before one Adam/repair step; OUT4 averages the physical TAIL parameters of four independent paths; SEQ4 spends the same four score calls on sequential updates of one path. SEQ4 uses learning rate 0.02 at every microstep and averages the final 64 macrostep-end states. OUT4 and SEQ4 match total score and update calls, not per-path optimization time or dynamics.

Mean observation joint KL is 0.0047217613 for IN4, 0.0019111960 for OUT4, and 0.0041083895 for SEQ4. The untrained identity reference is 0.0027110543; a same-data FACT quality reference achieves 0.0008839913. OUT4 improves over IN4 and identity under the original q5 simultaneous development intervals and precision gates. The OUT4-minus-identity difference is −0.0007998583, interval [−0.0012245702, −0.0003751463]. This new result does not retroactively add an identity test to the previous package.

Nevertheless, the original raw population score penalizes OUT4 relative to IN4: difference +0.0019532771, q2 interval [+0.0013296358, +0.0025769184]. SEQ4 has higher risk than OUT4 (+0.0021971935) but a lower raw score (−0.0008080227). Its score interval upper endpoint is only −0.0000053808, so this comparison is close to the inferential boundary. All five risk directions are resolved, but only four precision gates pass: SEQ4-minus-identity misses its prespecified SE threshold. Both score directions and precision gates pass; not all seven combined gates pass.

Each family uses alpha 0.025 and 16 independent outer units, df 15. Risk units include data and training randomness; score units additionally include independent per-data evaluation randomness. These are development t approximations under one fixed synthetic law and qualified numerical references, not cross-domain or exact-coverage guarantees.

The identity F_P=H(P)+R+J_P yields an OUT4-minus-IN4 expected log-estimation-bias difference of +0.0047638424, with a derived interval [+0.0025538601, +0.0069738247]. This accounting explains the reversed ordering without identifying optimizer causes or substituting a noise-variance scalar for the full likelihood distribution. It supplies no independent third experiment. FACT is a quality reference, not a cost-matched competitor or exact MLE; OUT4 is not claimed as a new averaging algorithm or universal recovery method.

The package contains 224 low-dimensional optimization programs and no neural-network fits. The sealed v7 manuscripts remain unchanged.
